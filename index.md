## Hello! 👋

I am a linguist💬 and programmer💻 working in Tsimshianic linguistics, endangered language technology, language revitalization, and data management.

* [Projects 🧰](/portfolio/tags)
* [Research Papers 📖](/papers)

My work has been centered around language technology and linguistic analysis for Indigenous language revitalization. Coming from a ten year career in academic research and fieldwork, in more recent times I have been happily involved in software development (data/backend) and language technology projects, and continue to support communities in their dedicated revitalization work.

I am always open to consult on Tsimshianic language matters and resource development, and am available to consult on technical projects centering the needs of endangered language communities. Currently I do a lot of work related to keyboards, fonts, and data migration/integrity. Reach out if you have projects that would benefit from an expert in the intersection of Indigenous languages and digital infrastructure!

## Place

I am from Edmonds ([Suquamish](https://suquamish.nsn.us/), [Stillaguamish](https://www.stillaguamish.com/), [Snohomish](https://snohomishtribe.org/)), and now live in Tucson ([Tohono O'odham](https://www.tonation-nsn.gov/), [Pasca Yaqui](https://www.pascuayaqui-nsn.gov/)). I have benefited immensely from my time living and working in Vancouver ([Musqueam](https://www.musqueam.bc.ca/), [Tsleil-Waututh](https://twnation.ca/), [Stó:lō](https://www.stolonation.bc.ca/)) and Toronto ([Wendat](https://www.wendake.ca/), [Seneca](https://www.sixnations.ca/), [Mississaugas of the Credit](https://mncfn.ca/)).
