A public language learning archive for Sgüüx̱s (Southern Tsimshian), the language of the Kitasoo people in Klemtu.

Sgüüx̱s is a sleeping language requiring revitalization from several decades of language recordings with the last fluent speaker, Violet Neasloss. My role involves data processing, resource development, translation, project management, and one-on-one language mentorship.
